East Forsyth Band Website & CMS Guide

Organization: East Forsyth Band Boosters / East Forsyth High School Band (Blue Regiment)
Public site: https://efhsband.org
Staff CMS: https://efhsband.org/admin
Document type: Live-site operations guide (PDF)
Last updated: August 16, 2026

Who this is for: Super Admins, editors, and booster officers who help run the band website. Any logged-in CMS user can open this PDF from the Dashboard Website Guide card.

Scope: This guide matches the features currently live on production. Planned tools that are not shipped yet (for example Treasurer Ledger, Gallery page, Notify Me push, or a separate Fundraising body editor) are omitted so staff are not pointed at menus that do not exist.

1. Overview

The East Forsyth Band website is a public community site plus a private Content Management System (CMS). The public site shows program information, calendar events, sponsors, fundraising, staff, boosters, home photo gallery, and contact forms. The CMS at /admin lets authorized staff edit that content, manage users, email staff, take officer Square Checkout payments, and keep meeting minutes.

Production runs on Cloudflare Pages with a Worker backend and a D1 database. CMS sessions last 24 hours. Saves in the CMS publish to the live public site.

2.1 Shared public features

  • Header branding: Public header shows the site logo, site title, and Blue Regiment mark.
  • Sponsor marquee: Logos/names of active Bronze, Silver, and Gold sponsors scroll sitewide under the header.
  • Homepage fly-in ad: A Silver/Gold (or homepage-ad) sponsor can briefly appear on Home. Timing is editable in CMS Manage sponsors settings (default about 6 seconds).
  • Home photo gallery: Photos uploaded in CMS Photos appear in the Home gallery section.
  • Maintenance mode: When enabled in Site Settings, public visitors are redirected to /maintenance.html. Super Admins can still open public pages and see a gold Super Admin preview banner at the top. Non–super-admin staff are treated like the public.

3. Public Payment Flows

3.1 Become a Sponsor (Square)

  1. Visitor chooses Bronze, Silver, or Gold (amounts and package copy are editable in CMS).
  2. They enter business name, address, phone, invoice email, and optional logo (image under about 2 MB).
  3. Square processes the card (in-popup Web Payments when Square is configured).
  4. On success the sponsor is activated on the public site in the correct tier, and an invoice email is sent when possible.

Package benefits (defaults):

  • Bronze: Website marquee logo feature
  • Silver: Marquee + homepage fly-in
  • Gold: Marquee + fly-in + game-day announcement recognition

3.2 Donate

Available from Sponsors and Fundraising. Visitor enters donor name and amount, then pays by Square card when checkout is configured.

3.3 Officer Checkout (CMS)

Treasurer, President, Vice President, and Super Admin can open Checkout in the CMS to key a card payment for a payer/entity, description, and amount. Minimum charge is $1.00. Card data stays with Square.

Square setup required for card forms: SQUARE_ACCESS_TOKEN, SQUARE_APPLICATION_ID (or SQUARE_APP_ID), optional SQUARE_LOCATION_ID, and SQUARE_ENVIRONMENT (sandbox or production).

4. CMS Login & Sessions

  • Login URL: https://efhsband.org/admin/login (also /admin)
  • Session length: 24 hours
  • Password: Minimum 8 characters. New users may be forced to change password on first login.
  • Change Password: Sidebar / mobile menu opens a toast form for every logged-in user.
  • Log Out: Sidebar (desktop) or mobile bar.
  • View Site: Dashboard button opens the public homepage in a new browser tab.

5. CMS Dashboard

After login, the Dashboard shows permission-filtered shortcut cards. Live cards include:

  • Website Guide (blue documentation card) — downloads this PDF
  • Checkout (green payments card) — Treasurer / President / Vice President / Super Admin
  • Staff Email
  • Meeting Minutes
  • Manage sponsors / Sponsors page / Become a Sponsor (when permitted)
  • Directors & Staff, Ensemble Body, Booster Members
  • Contact Form, User Management, Social / Facebook, Calendar Events

6. CMS Menu & Tools

6.1 Staff Email

Who can open it: Any logged-in CMS user.

Compose rich-text email to CMS users, with optional extra addresses and attachments. Reply-To uses the sender’s account username (should be a real email). Delivery uses the configured mail provider (Resend when connected).

6.2 Pages (visual editor)

Permission: pages for full page management, or page:<slug> for a specific page.

Edit live preview text with formatting (bold, italic, underline, color, size). Site admins can also change page settings (title, slug, path, layout, active flag). Unsaved changes warn before leaving.

Page shortcuts under Pages in the left nav appear for site admins. Editors with only a page permission usually open that page from a Manage shortcut (for example Ensemble).

6.3 Directors & Staff

Permission: staff or related directors page access.

Add/edit/delete staff members with name, role, bio, and photo. Drag rows to reorder the public directory.

6.4 Ensemble

Permission: page:ensembles (or pages).

Opens the Ensemble body editor for the Ensembles page content under the hero.

6.5 Band Boosters → Booster Members

Permission: boosters or Boosters page access.

Manage booster officer/member directory (photo, role, bio, drag reorder).

6.6 Band Boosters → Meeting Minutes

Who can view/print: Any logged-in CMS user.

Who can add / edit / upload .docx: minutes permission (labeled Meeting Minutes Secretary (add/edit)), plus Super Admin.

Who can delete: Super Admin only.

  • Choose a meeting date from the list to open the document in a floating viewer/print frame.
  • Secretaries use Add Minutes for the letterhead document editor, or Upload .docx to import a Word file (meeting date can come from the document’s Date field or the filename).
  • Non–Super Admin editors can edit only within 10 days of the meeting date (not from the upload/create time).
  • Canceling Add/Edit asks to confirm discarding unsaved changes. Closing View-only does not.
  • Minutes are staff-only; they are not published on the public Boosters page.

6.7 Calendar Events

Permission: events to create and manage your own events; events:manage to edit/delete any event.

Supports dates, rich descriptions, repeating events, exceptions, and booster-meeting placement. Public Calendar shows upcoming events only.

6.8 Sponsors → Manage sponsors

Permission: sponsors.

  • Add, edit, delete, and drag-reorder sponsor businesses and logos
  • Assign Bronze / Silver / Gold (controls marquee, fly-in, and public advertising)
  • Edit homepage fly-in timing
  • Print / download the Gold sponsors PDF sheet for advertising

Related page shortcuts: Sponsors page and Become a Sponsor edit the public page copy/package cards when the user has those page permissions.

6.9 Checkout

Permission: treasurer, president, or vice-president (Super Admin included).

Manual Square card charge for:

  1. User name or entity (required)
  2. Description of transaction (required)
  3. Amount (required, minimum $1.00)
  4. Optional internal note

Use this for in-person or phone keyed payments. Requires Square card checkout to be connected.

6.10 Contact Form

Permission: contact or Contact page access.

Manage contact topics and the email each topic delivers to. Review recent contact messages in the CMS.

6.11 Users (User Management)

Permission: users.

Create editor accounts, assign permissions, activate/disable users, and send welcome invites. Super Admins can grant Super Admin role.

6.12 Social / Facebook

Permission: site.

Connect the band Facebook Page through Zernio, then publish or schedule posts from the CMS when the integration is configured.

6.13 Site Settings

Permission: site (utility/home link editing may also be available with pages or home page access).

Edit site title, hero text, footer note, logo, maintenance mode, top utility links, and footer social links.

6.14 Photos

Permission: photos.

Upload, edit, delete, and drag-reorder gallery photos with alt text and captions. Uploaded photos appear in the Home photo gallery. Staff photos and sponsor logos are kept out of that public gallery listing.

Image uploads: about 2 MB server max (UI notes about 1.9 MB). Always include clear alt text.

7. Permissions Reference

Accounts are either Super Admin (role = admin) or Editor with an assigned permission list.

7.1 Super Admin

  • Has every permission automatically
  • Can delete meeting minutes
  • Can preview the live site during maintenance mode (gold banner)
  • Can use Checkout and all Manage tools

9. Email Features

  • Staff Email: internal CMS messaging with attachments
  • User welcome invites: sent when creating users (when mail is configured)
  • Contact form: topic-routed public messages
  • Sponsor invoices: sent from the Boosters invoice address after paid sponsorships when an invoice email is available

10. Photos & Media

  • Supported image types for Photos / logos / staff / booster / sponsor uploads: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, SVG
  • Server max about 2 MB per image
  • Always include alt text
  • Home gallery shows public gallery photos; utility uploads (staff photos, sponsor logos) are excluded from that listing
  • Staff Email attachments are separate from the image upload limit

11. Facebook / Zernio (optional)

With site permission and a configured Zernio API key, staff can connect the band Facebook Page and publish or schedule posts from Social / Facebook.

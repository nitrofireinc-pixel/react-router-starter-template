Band Boosters

Pay band dues

Families can pay student band dues with a credit card through Square. You will enter the student’s full name, the amount being paid, and an email address for the receipt.

  • Successful payments email a paid receipt and add a dues entry to the treasurer ledger.
  • Failed payments email a failed receipt and add a failed dues ledger record for tracking.
